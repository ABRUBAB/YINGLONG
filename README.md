# YINGLONG

**Personal Agent Identity & Mandated Finance**

*Always with you. Never beyond your mandate.*

YINGLONG gives a person or a business a verifiable AI agent that can act on their money, but only as them, only inside the mandate they sealed, and always with proof. A mandate is a digital power of attorney, enforced by code: the user states rules in plain language, YINGLONG compiles them into an ERC-7710 delegation whose caveat enforcers are the rules, the user seals it with a passkey, and the same enforcer bytecode runs on a public chain (Base Sepolia) and on a FISCO BCOS consortium chain.

Entry for the 2026 Shenzhen International FinTech Competition, International Track, Topic F (Agent Wallet).
